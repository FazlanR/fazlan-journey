More Than
a Job Title.

I've spent more than a decade in hospitality. Long enough to know that the job is only part of the story. The real education happens in the pressure, the late hours, the difficult moments with people — and the occasional perfect evening when everything falls into place.

I'm Sri Lankan. That's not just a nationality — it's a way of seeing the world that I carry with me wherever I go. I left, but I never stopped belonging there.

I've lived in Singapore for over a decade. Built a career here, built relationships here, and learned more about independence than I expected. Singapore made me sharper. Sri Lanka made me human. Both made me who I am.

What drives me now is the idea of building something that belongs to me — something rooted in Sri Lankan hospitality, built with the discipline and standards I developed here. Selah Ceylon Hospitality Group is that vision taking shape.

This website is my attempt to document the journey honestly. Not as a success story. Just as a life, still in progress.
