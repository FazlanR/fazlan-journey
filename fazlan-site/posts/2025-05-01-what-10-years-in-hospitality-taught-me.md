Ten years is long enough to stop romanticising something. Long enough to see it clearly — the beauty and the cost, the things it builds in you and the things it quietly takes away.

What I know now is that hospitality isn't really about food or drinks or service in the technical sense. It's about reading people. Understanding what they need before they know how to ask for it. Creating an environment where someone can exhale.

That's harder than it sounds. And it takes years to learn.
